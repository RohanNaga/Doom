D. The reconstruction upper bound: every place it appears

The literature answer. The game world-model papers do not report one: GameNGen fine-tunes its decoder but gives no reconstruction number, DIAMOND is pixel-space and has none, MultiGen and Matrix-Game report absolute scores only. The papers that do report a reconstruction ceiling are the latent-forecasting and physics-world-model ones we cite for it: OccWorld, DINO-Foresight, and Kang et al. (ICML 2025), who use it exactly as we do, to show the error sits in prediction, not reconstruction. Two judges also noted that "bound" is the wrong word: it is an empirical reference, not a proven maximum.
