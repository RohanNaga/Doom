## Questions

1. **Read the curves.** For each arena: the zero-shot A, the A at 4,000, the half-gap crossing (or censored), the shape (where most of the gain lands; whether it has flattened by 4,000 or is still rising), and the latent skill and LPIPS beside it. Which arenas cross, which do not, and does the ordering relate to anything (zero-shot A, distance D, motion, persistence PSNR)? Compute Spearman correlations with the frozen per-arena table where it helps, and say what they mean with n = 13.
2. **Is this the right recipe?** Judge against what the curves show and the literature: is 4,000 updates enough (are curves flat)? Is 8 episodes enough (the data ladder 1/2/4/8/16 has not run)? Would rank, alpha (= 2r), including the MLP, a higher or lower learning rate, more warmup, or EMA-scored weights plausibly change the picture, and which single change is worth a two-arena test tomorrow? Is there any sign of overfitting to the 8 episodes (held-out A falling while training loss falls; latent ratio diverging)? What evaluation would settle each doubt, at what cost (A4000-hours)?
3. **What else should be measured before the battery.** The forgetting guard (training-map validation at each checkpoint), the directional guard, the perceptual margin against raw persistence and the raw-PSNR columns (Sunday's rescore), the EMA weights, a second seed, the data ladder on four arenas, the full fine-tune comparator. Rank them by what they add to the paper's claim and by cost. Name the evals you would want to look at with your own eyes (curves, per-arena tables, a few decoded frames before and after adaptation).
4. **Rohan's "base LoRA" question.** Should there be a control that applies the same LoRA recipe to the base pretrained SD 1.4 (no four-map pretraining) on an arena, to show that the four-map pretraining is what the adapter builds on? Its control path would be untrained, so it is a different regime; say whether it is worth one arena for the Sep 30 paper, for October, or not at all, and what it would show.
5. **The battery.** Given the recipe you would settle on, list the runs for the paper (arenas × seeds × backbones × comparators) with A4000- or A6000-hours, what must be in the Sep 30 draft and what is October, and the order to run them so the most informative results land first.
