# Astra literature pass: what successful transformer world models actually inherit

Sep 14, 2026. Independent follow-up to the DiT-gap review, requested by Rohan. Recommendations below are not adopted decisions. No server execution, queue edits or training-code changes. Primary papers, official repositories and released configurations were read over HTTP; configuration-only parameter counts were computed locally using PERSEVE Python and meta-device diffusers models.

## Conclusions

1. Strong interactive video results do not establish that replacing a U-Net with an ImageNet DiT under channel-stacked context will improve this small-data Doom experiment. Several leading systems inherit trained video transformers, use temporal token sequences, and separately train controls and long-horizon generation. They are not architecture-only comparisons. Some disclose too little to establish initialization or training cost. In particular, do not label Genie 3 a verified DiT or claim Oasis was video-pretrained without a source.
2. Our corpus is about 33.5 hours at 35 tics/s and four tics/decision (1,054,647 decision frames, before train split), not millions of independent videos. 90k x 32 is 2.88M sampled target exposures. Verified game adaptation corpora below range from about 400 hours to thousands of hours, on top of foundation pretraining where applicable. The papers support pretraining/data/context design as plausible explanations, not a controlled causal proof of why transformers win.
3. Under the new request to address the pretraining objection, favor a full **PixArt-alpha 512 third-backbone run** over the queued second ImageNet DiT seed, after a one-hour implementation/fit gate and agreement. It preserves the data, SD VAE, channel context, objective and update budget while testing a broader pretrained transformer package. It is not a video-pretraining experiment and does not isolate corpus from architecture. Open-Sora 1.1 is the best verified compatible candidate for a later native-video experiment; do not strip its temporal layers and call the resulting initialization video modeling.
4. Correct the earlier inventory: compatible larger pretrained transformers include **Large-DiT 3B/7B, FiTv2-3B and DiT-MoE up to 16.5B**, as well as UniDiffuser 1B. No claim that no larger compatible model exists remains justified. They are different architectures from original DiT-XL/2, and have serious memory/compute costs. Three billion parameters alone require about 48GB for fp32 weights, gradients and two Adam moments, before activations or EMA; ordinary full fine-tuning on one shared A6000 is not a practical default.

## Verified world-model recipes

### Oasis (official October 2024 release)

- Official release calls the public model a **500M** downscaled checkpoint; live demo uses a larger checkpoint of undisclosed size. Spatial/temporal transformer with causal temporal attention; autoregressive frames.
- Initialization, exact training dataset size and training GPU-hours are **not disclosed in the verified official release material**. Do not substitute VPT's dataset size for Oasis's consumed training set, and do not infer scratch training just because no initializer is named.
- Custom **ViT-VAE-L/20**, checkpoint `vit-l-20.safetensors`, not SD KL-f8. Official post: "The model was trained using Diffusion Forcing"; independent per-token/frame noise and dynamic inference noising. Inference code explicitly uses velocity output: `x_start = sqrt(alpha) * x_curr - sqrt(1-alpha) * v`.
- Sources: https://oasis-model.github.io/ ; https://github.com/etched-ai/open-oasis/tree/master ; https://huggingface.co/Etched/oasis-500m ; `dit.py`, `generate.py` in that repo. Links to the old Decart/Etched detailed blogs returned 404 during this pass.

### Matrix-Game 2.0 (2508.13009)

- Paper: "initialize our model with SkyReels-V2-I2V-1.3B", following Wan2.1 architecture. Add action modules to reach **1.8B**. It is not simply a Wan-T2V checkpoint.
- Wan2.1 causal 3D VAE, spatial compression 8x8 and temporal 4x. Flow-matching output confirmed in released `utils/wan_wrapper.py`: `pred = noise - x0`. Bidirectional teacher converted to block-causal student, KV caches and separately injected mouse/keyboard actions.
- Foundation: 5k initial adaptation, then reported **120k steps**, **batch256**, **lr2e-5**; **40k ODE pairs**, **6k** causal initialization, **4k** DMD self-forcing, **lr6e-6**; latent chunk length3, attention local size6. Inference 25fps on one H100 is not training cost. Aggregate GPU-hours not stated.
- Preserve the paper's data distinctions: abstract says "~1200 hours"; detailed foundation section says about 800h (153h Minecraft + 615h Unreal) plus **85h Sekai**; **574h GTA** and **560h Temple Run** are additional specialized fine-tuning data. Do not collapse these into a single exact total shared by every released checkpoint.
- Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.13009 ; https://github.com/SkyworkAI/Matrix-Game/tree/main/Matrix-Game-2 ; https://github.com/SkyworkAI/Matrix-Game/blob/main/Matrix-Game-2/utils/wan_wrapper.py

### GameGen-X (2411.00769)

- Masked spatial-temporal DiT plus InstructNet. Foundation trained for text-to-video and continuation on OGameData-GEN; foundation frozen during control-module instruction tuning. **Parameter count and named external initializer are not disclosed in the inspected paper/repo.** Do not infer CogVideoX initialization from its use as a baseline.
- Paper Table1: **"1000k" clips, "4000h"**, 150+ games. Official release distinguishes **860k generation** and **140k instruction** examples.
- 3D spatial-temporal VAE; exact checkpoint/encoder identity not specified in the inspected text. Spatial and temporal attention, with prefix frames excluded from noising/denoising. Rectified flow stated explicitly. This is temporal prefix conditioning, not flattening a history into one image's channel dimension; not necessarily causal attention.
- The **8 H100 GPUs** statement occurs in the appendix's **20k-generation/10k-instruction ablation** setup (3 and 2 epochs). It is not a measured full-training GPU-day budget. Full cost not established.
- Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.00769 ; https://github.com/GameGen-X/GameGen-X

### Hunyuan-GameCraft 1.0 (2506.17201)

- Initializes from **HunyuanVideo**, a **13B-class** MM-DiT foundation model. The downstream exact adapter-inclusive count is not separately quoted in the inspected paper. HunyuanVideo causal 3D VAE, 16 latent channels, compression4 temporal /8 spatial.
- More than **1M six-second clips**, 100+ AAA games, plus about **3,000** high-quality rendered motion sequences. Multiplying clips by six seconds gives >1,667 clip-hours, a derived exposure-duration figure rather than a unique-recording-hours claim.
- Rectified-flow/flow-matching generation, hybrid history conditioning: image, last latent, or historical clip. Clean head and noisy continuation are temporal latent segments, with additional condition/mask channels; do not claim channel concatenation is wholly absent. Chunk-wise autoregression need not mean causal attention inside each chunk. Separate acceleration/distillation stage.
- Paper: **"192 NVIDIA H20 GPUs"**, full-parameter training, **batch48**, **30k** iterations at **3e-5**, then **20k** at **1e-5**. History ratios 0.7 single clip /0.05 multiple clips /0.25 single frame; **33 frames**, **720p**, **25fps**. No elapsed duration establishing GPU-days.
- Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.17201 ; https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan-GameCraft-1.0 ; https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanVideo

### The Matrix (2412.03568)

- **2.3B pretrained video DiT +0.4B interactive modules =2.7B**. The exact named foundation checkpoint is not disclosed in the inspected text; do not silently label it CogVideoX or Open-Sora.
- Source: **750k labeled +1.2M unlabeled six-second clips**, initially60fps and downsampled16fps. Derived total clip duration3,250h (1,250 labeled +2,000 unlabeled), not necessarily unique recording hours.
- Video VAE represents four decoded frames per temporal latent; exact VAE identity not established. Temporal token stream; causal action-to-video cross-attention, shift-window denoising with differing noise levels, consistency-model distillation. Do not relabel this self-forcing or assign a v/epsilon target not disclosed by the inspected material.
- **20k** LoRA adaptation (rank128), **20k** interactive-module training with backbone frozen, **60k** full Swin-DPM training, **10k** consistency distillation; **batch32**, **lr1e-5**, bf16,720p16fps. **8 GPUs** for long-sequence parallelism is stated; total GPU type/days not established.
- Task distinction: data pipeline removes HUDs, filters collisions; Cyberpunk collection disables NPCs and moving vehicles. This is not an equivalent combat-state prediction benchmark.
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.03568 , including appendix A.2/B.

### Yume 1.0 (2507.17744)

- **SkyReels-V2-14B-540P** initialization, Wan architecture/VAE lineage; masked video DiT and compressed history tokens, rectified-flow/ReFlow training, sampling refinements and adversarial distillation. Not a verified self-forcing training recipe for version1.0.
- Training source **400h Sekai-Real-HQ** selected from6,620h Sekai-Real; quantized-motion extraction produces **139,019 clips**. Do not call the larger raw collection its final training set.
- **7,000 iterations**, **batch40**, **lr1e-5**, **544x960**, **16fps**, A800 GPUs; paper does not give count/elapsed GPU-hours. Repository's >=16A100 training requirement is a separate implementation requirement, not measured cost.
- History uses FramePack-like downsampling/patchification of temporal context, not32 frames collapsed into channels. 33-frame prediction chunks; 30% short-history sampling /70%400–800-frame history sampling in the described memory strategy.
- Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17744 ; https://github.com/stdstu12/YUME ; https://huggingface.co/stdstu123/Yume-I2V-540P

### Yume 1.5 (2512.22096, distinct newer recipe)

- **Wan2.2-TI2V-5B** initialization and its causal Wan2.2 VAE (4 temporal,16x16 spatial compression), not SD latents. Adds text-controlled generation and self-forcing with TSCM.
- Sekai-Real-HQ-derived exploration plus **50k synthetic videos retained from80k** generated by Wan2.1-14B using selected OpenVid captions. Do not confuse the synthetic teacher with the5B initializer.
- **10k foundation iterations**, then **600** self-forcing/TSCM iterations, **batch40**, **lr1e-5**, **704x1280**,16fps, A100s; no aggregate training GPU-hours reported in inspected details.
- Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.22096 ; https://github.com/stdstu12/YUME ; https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2

### Cosmos 1.0 diffusion family (2501.03575; explicit version)

- **7B and14B** video diffusion foundation models. Own large-scale foundation pretraining; Video2World is adapted from Text2World. No Wan/Hunyuan external initialization is specified. Do not confuse this diffusion family with Cosmos's separately trained GPT/autoregressive family, or infer details for later Cosmos releases.
- **20M hours raw source videos**, curated into **~100M pretraining clips** (2–60s), plus~10M fine-tuning clips and image data. The raw-hours figure is not all consumed training video.
- Custom causal **Cosmos-1.0-Tokenizer-CV8x8x8**, not SD VAE. EDM-preconditioned denoising score matching / weighted clean-sample regression, **not simply our VP v-loss**. 3D patch tokens and full spatiotemporal self-attention. Video2World concatenates observations along **time**, augments conditioning noise, and includes a condition indicator.
- Report: **"10,000 NVIDIA H100 GPUs"** over **"three months"** for **all WFM models reported**, not for one7B checkpoint. No per-model breakdown should be invented.
- Action-conditioned Bridge post-training is documented: an action MLP is added to timestep embedding. This is relevant precedent for conditioning, but does not supply a matched U-Net-vs-DiT causal comparison.
- Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.03575 , sections3/5/6.

### Genie 3

- Official announcement reports **720p,24fps**, consistency for minutes. Architecture, parameter count, initial checkpoint, VAE, objective, training corpus and training compute are not disclosed there. Therefore exclude it from the table of *verified DiT training recipes*. Its capabilities do not substantiate a specific reproducible DiT recipe.
- Source: https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/

## Verified SD1.x checkpoint families and warm-start ranking

Interpret exact latent compatibility as the **same fixed SD1.x KL-f8 encoder coordinates and0.18215 scaling**, not merely four channels. Official ft-EMA/ft-MSE card explicitly says only the decoder was fine-tuned. We verified declared encoder provenance/configuration, not cryptographic equality of every checkpoint's encoder tensors. Before a new run, compare encoder+quant_conv weights or deterministic posterior means on identical images; check preprocessing and scaling too. Current Doom latent input is132x32x40;16x20 is the **patch-token grid**.

Ranked for this deadline, grouping release variants rather than attempting an exhaustive census of all third-party fine-tunes:

1. **PixArt-alpha (256/512/1024, SAM and aesthetic/COCO variants)**: ~0.6B; local diffusers512 config count **610,856,096** before Doom modifications. ImageNet initialization,10M SAM captions,14M HQ (4M JourneyDB+10M internal); paper total25M images across stages. Prefer standard512. Native cross-attention action token, timestep-path bucket,132-channel inflation, rectangular position handling,4-channel velocity head. No T5 needed for learned action embeddings. Broad image prior, no video prior. Source https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.00426 ; https://github.com/PixArt-alpha/PixArt-alpha ; https://huggingface.co/PixArt-alpha/PixArt-XL-2-512x512 . Alpha's LCM/Delta and DMD derivative weights also exist in this family; prefer undistilled weights for full velocity retraining. Delta: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.05252 ; https://huggingface.co/PixArt-alpha/PixArt-LCM-XL-2-1024-MS .
2. **Open-Sora1.1 / STDiT2**, released stage2/stage3, reported~700M: **9.7M videos+2.6M images** pretraining, **560k videos+1.6M images** fine-tuning; PixArt-alpha1024 lineage. ## Adaptation and one-hour gates

**Latent contract, first:** compare deterministic encoded means and encoder/quant-conv weights, preprocessing and scale; same raw tensors/seed should reconstruct through our frozen encoder/decoder contract. Merely seeing4channels/0.18215 in configuration is insufficient for a previously unknown VAE.

**Alpha gate:** load transformer only; verify checkpoint load report; check132-channel target-last projection, correctly indexed4-channel output, action/noise dependence, finite backward/update and a small real-batch learning test. Benchmark actual AdamW/bf16/globalbatch32 with microbatch4 or8 and accumulation. A one-hour pilot includes setup; fail closed on an unreviewed wrapper, not on unfavorable early validation. Proposed new options (not implemented): `--backbone pixart --warm-start PixArt-alpha/PixArt-XL-2-512x512`. Existing recipe values remain `--context-frames 32 --lr 5e-5 --warmup 2000 --global-batch 32 --action-dropout 0 --steps 90000 --require-verified-transitions`; seed0. Preserve noise0.7,clip1,wd0,split,corruption and evaluation. Forecast90k from measured updates/s, protect >=8–12hours for evaluation, and compare both equal-exposure and equal-compute endpoints. No queue replacement until discussion/agreement.

**Native-video gate (Open-Sora1.1 first, then Latte):** reconstruct `B,4,T,32,40` from channel-stacked context plus noisy target; retain frame tokens/temporal layers, predict last target only, use observed-history mask and correct per-frame timestep treatment. No future ground-truth frames are passed. For single-next-frame prediction, existing bidirectional attention over observed history and the noisy target is not future leakage; introducing strict causal masks is optional modeling surgery, not mandatory correctness. Action refers only to the transition into the target; do not broadcast it as if every historical transition used that action. Add bucket conditioning for history corruption. Test T=9 and33, identicalL32-eligible target IDs, memory and actual optimizer-update time atglobalbatch32. Current L32 gives33x320=10,560 tokens versus320; factorized temporal attention avoids full10,560-squared attention but still runs spatial processing over33frames. Do not claim a precise33x end-to-end slowdown without measurement. A one-frame call discards the temporal transfer rationale.

**Other image-model gate:** checkpoint/encoder load, positions, output/noise convention and native sampling smoke first; then one real backward pass after132-channel adaptation and verify action/noise gradients. SiT/REPA/FiTv2/MoE flow models need explicit time/target conversion or deliberate retraining; the word velocity is not compatibility. Larger models must fit complete optimizer state, not inference weights only. Do not spend a day on a new distributed stack this week.

**What is reproducible in96A6000-hours?** Broad pretrained image transformer adaptation; a small feasibility test of a compatible video transformer; native action conditioning; diagnostics and honest compute-matched evaluation. Token-based temporal context is implementable but adds a major compute/recipe change. Full modern video foundation pretraining, hundreds/thousands of hours of curated controllable game data, or full self-forcing distillation (generator plus teacher/fake-score models and on-policy rollout machinery) are not credible additions to this deadline. Superficially matching one technique does not reproduce the successful system.

## Proposed claim and one run

Provisional: "Under a shared channel-conditioned next-frame recipe and limited adaptation budget, an ImageNet-initialized DiT-XL/2 does not outperform an SD-initialized U-Net." After a budgetedAlpha third row, discuss sensitivity to the pretrained transformer package. This is not evidence that video-pretrained transformers lose, nor that transformers inherently need more data. If Alpha closes the gap, initialization/architecture package matters; if it does not, broaden only to the two tested transformer packages. Both outcomes are useful. Rollouts/paired episode intervals and compute accounting remain necessary.

This changes the earlier extra-slot recommendation from seed1 toAlpha **for the now-explicit pretraining objection**. Seed1 remains best for repeatability of the original narrow two-package comparison; it does not investigate that objection. If Rohan specifically chooses video-pretraining as the new research question, the appropriate next run is native Open-Sora1.1 with a measured compute cap and openly different context representation, not a covert change to the original pair.
