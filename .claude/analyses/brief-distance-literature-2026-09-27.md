## What the numbers say (recompute, do not trust)

- D separates the families (training maps' validation episodes 0.026 to 0.069; every unseen arena 0.118 to 0.270) and orders nothing within the 13 unseen arenas (|Spearman| <= 0.42 against zero-shot advantage, endpoint, gain, half-gap budget; p >= 0.15; leave-one-out no better than the mean; unchanged partial on motion and persistence PSNR).
- Coverage and G do not even separate the families, track motion (persistence PSNR -0.69 and +0.63), and G's neighbour predictor is worse than copy-last on the training maps.
- The frozen model's zero-shot latent skill S0 orders the arenas: Spearman +0.90 with the advantage at 4k, -0.61 with the budget; PixArt's zero-shot advantage ranks the arenas like the U-Net's (+0.95), so the ordering is a property of the footage. The arenas all belong to one WAD (shared textures, engine, enemies); they differ in layout, motion and control statistics. Home-to-fresh scene PSNR drops are 2.2 to 2.7 dB for all three backbones.
- The adaptation ceiling per arena is set by the arena: one adaptation episode gives most of the gain, and lr or budget changes stay inside seed noise.
