All three recomputed the tuned rows independently and agree to the third decimal (IQM of A 2.03 at 0, 3.79 at 4k, arena-bootstrap [3.51, 4.26]; crossed fraction 0.69 [0.46, 0.92]; median budget 1k; S0 vs A4k 0.90, vs budget −0.61; LPIPS lower on 11 of 13, median M −0.021).

## Agreed by all three (adopted)

1. **Unit and claim.** The arena is the unit (n = 13, every unseen arena the WAD has). Headline claims describe the distribution across these arenas (interquartile mean, quartiles, fraction by budget) with arena-bootstrap intervals; per-arena statements carry the episode interval; the "a typical new arena of this game" reading is stated as an explicit within-WAD assumption, and nothing is claimed beyond the WAD.
2. **Figure 4, body.** (a) the interquartile-mean learning curve across arenas with a nested (arena, then episode) bootstrap band and the 13 per-arena lines faint behind it, home dashed, log x with the break for 0; (b) the cumulative attainment curve, one minus Kaplan–Meier, "fraction of arenas that have reached their half-gap line" against updates, censored at the last grid point, with an at-risk row under the axis and per-arena ticks; the fixed threshold A >= 3.5 dB as a second thin curve. Appendix: the 13 small multiples with episode bands, the performance profiles at 0, 500 and 4k, the endpoint-against-skill (or deficit) scatter, the seed and ladder panels.
3. **Statistics in the text.** Every aggregate carries its arena-bootstrap interval; the median budget is replaced by the attainment curve because resampling arenas moves it from 250 to beyond 4k; the share of the gap closed is reported as a continuous number (median 0.58, quartiles 0.47 to 0.73) beside the binary count, because home itself has an interval (5.06 [4.75, 5.39]) at whose low end all 13 cross; "tie" is dropped from the LPIPS claim (no equivalence margin was set in advance): lower on 11 of 13, the other two within 0.01; the ladder gap reads "about 0.25 dB" (arena 12: 0.253); S0 predicts where an arena ends up (0.90), not how much it gains (0.18).
4. **Runs.** Finish the queued 8k grid, its seeds and guards; the figure repair needs no new training. Opus's cross-arena control (each adapter scored on another arena, 40 GPU-minutes) is queued behind the chain as a check that adapters learn the arena and not the recording protocol. Seed 1 on the other nine arenas (22 GPU-hours) is Rohan's call for tonight.
5. **Pitfalls named and answered.** The line depends on A0 (report fixed-budget A and AUC beside it; never correlate the budget with A0); arenas share a WAD (the population sentence); one seed (the seed panel, spread a tenth of the between-arena spread); coarse grid and the tie at 250 (the 8k grid's early points); "9 of 13" is fragile (the four non-crossers miss by 0.08 to 0.15 dB; the interval and the share of gap closed say so).
6. **The other figures.** Rollouts: matched rows per map, tic-0 context column, tuned decoder, scene crop, per-tic PSNR under the model rows. Family step: numeric D axis, floor band, no fitted line, "a step, not a slope". Per-arena zero-shot: paired stock-to-tuned dot plot sorted by the ordering quantity (Opus: A0; main: S0; either is defensible, S0 chosen since the text names it). Training curves: advantage over copy-last against updates, linear x. Decoder pair: raw, stock, tuned in one row with a scene zoom. Distance null: one sentence plus the appendix table. Tables: reference rows first, intervals instead of bold.
