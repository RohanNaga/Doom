# H100 rental providers — evidence card, read 2026-09-22

Decision: rent one 8×H100 node for ~5 days (Sep 22–27 2026, 120 h = 960 GPU-hours) to run two
diffusion world-model trainings concurrently (SD 1.4 U-Net 0.86 B, batch 32; SD 3.5 Medium 2.25 B,
batch 32 + grad checkpointing), a VAE encode of ~10 M frames at 320×256, and evaluation.
Budget target $2,500–$3,500. Data: public HF dataset, 0.6 TB needed first, 2.75 TB total, must land
on local NVMe. Checkpoints ~0.4 TB. PyTorch 2.x + diffusers, one env, no Slurm.

Every number below carries its source URL. All pages read 2026-09-22 unless noted.
Anything I could not confirm from a primary page is marked **not stated** or **VERIFY**.

## 2. Per-provider detail

### Lambda — [lambda.ai/pricing](https://lambda.ai/pricing), [docs.lambda.ai](https://docs.lambda.ai/public-cloud/on-demand/)

- **Price**: $3.99/GPU-hr for the 8×H100 SXM instance; $4.29 for 1×, $4.19 for 2×, $4.09 for 4×.
  H100 PCIe 1× is $3.29.
- **Node spec (8×)**: 80 GB VRAM/GPU, 208 vCPU, 1,800 GiB RAM, **22 TiB SSD**. That one number
  settles the data problem: the full 2.75 TB dataset plus 0.4 TB of checkpoints fits on the
  instance's own volume with room to spare.
- **1-Click Clusters**: $6.16/GPU-hr at 16 GPUs, minimum term **2 weeks** — wrong shape for a
  5-day run and 54% more per GPU-hour. Ignore it.
- **Minimum commitment**: none stated for on-demand. Billing granularity for instances is **not
  stated** on the docs page I read; filesystems are "billed per GB used per month, in increments
  of one hour".
- **Access**: VM, SSH; the docs reference "the ubuntu user's home directory", so the login user is
  `ubuntu` with sudo, not root. Bare metal is Private Cloud, not on-demand.
- **Persistent storage**: filesystem price is **not stated** on the pricing page. The billing doc
  shows "$0.20 per GiB per month" and explicitly says "The rate above is used for example purposes
  and might not reflect current pricing"
  ([docs.lambda.ai/public-cloud/billing](https://docs.lambda.ai/public-cloud/billing/)). Treat as
  VERIFY in console.
- **Egress**: "There is no minimum storage period, and you are not charged for ingress or egress."
  (same billing doc). Inbound speed from HF: **not stated**.
- **Billing**: card on file, US regions only.
- **Availability risk**: Lambda H100 inventory has historically sold out at peak; third-party
  reviews in 2026 still list "availability constraints during peak demand" and no spot tier
  ([thundercompute.com/blog/lambda-labs-alternatives](https://www.thundercompute.com/blog/lambda-labs-alternatives),
  secondary). Check the console before committing.
- **Academic**: Lambda Research Grant Program, up to **$5,000** in cloud credits, rolling
  competitive applications, expanded at CVPR 2026 to cover research groups (secondary:
  [grantedai.com](https://grantedai.com/grants/lambda-research-grant-program-cloud-gpu-credits-ai-ml-researchers-lambda-a7b8c9d0)).
  No same-day path stated.

### RunPod — [runpod.io/pricing](https://www.runpod.io/pricing)

- **Price**: Secure Cloud H100 SXM $3.49, H100 NVL $3.19, H100 PCIe $2.89. Community Cloud
  $2.69 / $2.59 / $1.99 respectively.
- **Storage**: container disk $0.10/GB/mo; network volume $0.07/GB/mo under 1 TB, $0.05/GB/mo over
  1 TB; high-performance network volume $0.14/GB/mo. 2.75 TB on a standard network volume is about
  **$138/month**, i.e. ~$23 for five days.
- **Egress**: **not stated** on the pricing page. Secondary sources assert RunPod charges nothing
  for ingress or egress, but also warn that on Community Cloud "underlying network usage at some
  community hosts may apply"
  ([northflank.com/blog/runpod-gpu-pricing](https://northflank.com/blog/runpod-gpu-pricing),
  secondary). VERIFY before pushing 2.75 TB through a Community host.
- **Access**: containers, not bare VMs. You get SSH into a pod; you are root inside the container,
  but you do not own the host kernel or the NVMe layout.
- **Minimum commitment**: none; per-second-ish billing on pods.
- **Community Cloud caveat**: machines are third-party hosted. For a 5-day continuous run the
  host can disappear and the 80 GB/GPU claim varies by listing. This is the cheap-but-fragile tier.
- **Billing**: prepaid credits by card.

## 4. Recommendation

**Two best for "start within hours, one 8×H100 node, 5 days, $2,500–$3,500":**

1. **Lambda on-demand 8×H100 SXM — $31.92/hr, $3,830 for 120 h.** Slightly over the top of the
   band, and the best fit on every non-price axis: a single 8-GPU VM with **22 TiB of local SSD**
   (the only option where 2.75 TB of data plus 0.4 TB of checkpoints is a non-issue), ordinary SSH
   with sudo, no ingress/egress charge, no minimum term, card billing, no quota request. Buy 4
   GPUs' worth of run each and both trainings sit on one node. The risk is availability: check the
   console first, and if 8× is gone, 2× or 4× at $4.19/$4.09 is a worse deal per GPU but still
   launches. To land inside budget, run ~100 h instead of 120 h ($3,192) or accept $3,830.
2. **Nebius on-demand — $30.80/hr, $3,696 for 120 h.** Same order of price, free egress and free
   public IP, generous 16 vCPU / 200 GB RAM per GPU, self-serve card signup, managed Slurm free if
   you ever want it. The catch is that local NVMe is a separately-billed block volume at
   $0.065/GiB/mo (~$38 for 3.5 TB over five days, immaterial) and the per-node NVMe spec is not
   published, so confirm the disk attaches at NVMe speed before you start the encode.

**Cheapest that actually meets the need: RunPod Secure Cloud H100 SXM at $27.92/hr, $3,350 for
120 h.** Comfortably inside budget, self-serve, per-second billing, dedicated (not community)
hardware. Add ~$23 for a 2.75 TB network volume. Accept that you are in a container rather than on
a VM, and confirm the pod's local disk is NVMe and large enough before committing the encode to it.

If the budget is the binding constraint rather than the schedule, **SF Compute at ~$2.07/GPU-hr
(~$1,987 for five days)** is the real bargain and its price has been flat within 10 cents all
week — but its `sea-3-h100` SKU carries only **1.5 TiB of storage**, under the 2.75 TB total, so
you would have to stage the dataset in two passes or attach external storage. That is a real
engineering cost against a ~$1,400 saving.

**What to avoid, and why:**

- **Any spot or interruptible tier for a 5-day run.** Nebius preemptible ($2,064), CoreWeave spot
  ($2,365), Azure spot ($2,180) and Vast.ai interruptible (~$960) all look tempting. A preemption
  at hour 90 of a 120-hour training costs more in wall-clock and babysitting than the $1,500 saved,
  unless checkpoint-and-resume is already proven end-to-end. It is not worth it for a run that
  feeds an October paper deadline.
- **AWS p5, GCP A3 and Azure ND H100 v5 on-demand.** At $55–$98/hr per node they are 2–3× the
  budget ($6,605 / $10,619 / $11,798), and all three gate 8×H100 behind a quota increase that
  routinely takes days. Reference only.
- **CoreWeave.** The on-demand node at $49.24/hr is over budget, and signup appears to be
  sales-gated, which kills a same-day start.
- **Lambda 1-Click Clusters.** $6.16/GPU-hr with a two-week minimum — 54% more per GPU-hour than
  Lambda's own on-demand 8× node, for a commitment three times longer than the run.
- **Vast.ai and RunPod Community Cloud.** Cheapest posted numbers in the table, but third-party
  hosts, per-listing storage and bandwidth terms, and no guarantee the machine survives five
  continuous days. Community hosts may also meter the 2.75 TB download.
- **Hyperbolic.** No published price, and the rate it is reported at more than doubled inside ten
  weeks. You cannot plan a budget against it.
- **Prime Intellect and Voltage Park.** Both plausibly cheap (~$1,700–$1,900 for five days) and
  both self-serve, but neither publishes a price you can act on without logging in. Worth five
  minutes in each console before committing to Lambda — if Voltage Park quotes near $1.99/GPU-hr
  bare metal with InfiniBand and no minimum term, it beats everything on this list. Do not plan
  around it sight-unseen.
