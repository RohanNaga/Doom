# Distance literature round: two views and the synthesis (2026-09-27, 10:00 EDT)

Sources: `opus-distance-literature-2026-09-27.md` (Opus 5.5 high, worktree commit 4b8e07e), `astra-distance-literature-2026-09-27.md` (Astra, thread 01a0e2f7-77b5-7d42-b252-2bac0453e7ef), brief `brief-distance-literature-2026-09-27.md`; the morning three-way `distance-usefulness-decision-2026-09-27.md`. Both recomputed the tables; both reproduce `paper/tables/tuned/adapt_summary.json`. Rohan's ask: "It would be nice if we had a distance which nicely showed the domain shift."

## Where the two agree

1. **The literature has no distance that predicts adaptation cost for dynamics or video models.** Distances predict transfer in three situations only: the shift range is wide (OTDD 2002.02923, Westny 2606.30777 across 24 datasets), difficulty is fixed by construction (XEWorld 2608.05799 holds the scene and varies the robot), or the distance lives in the model's own representation (LEEP 2002.12462, LogME 2102.11005). We have none of the three. With the next frame as a free label, every model-side transferability score reduces to the zero-shot skill S0.
2. **The null is a construct problem, not noise.** D's between-arena spread is ten times its episode-subset spread; outcome reliability is 0.93 to 0.97; the seed spread is a tenth of the between-arena spread. The arenas are ordered by their intrinsic predictability, and that order persists through adaptation (S0 vs S4000 0.85), is shared by the backbones (PixArt 0.95, SD 3.5 0.81), and is orthogonal to D (−0.05). A dataset distance measures novelty; the outcome mixes novelty with difficulty, and difficulty wins within one WAD.
3. **The shift is real and visible, and D shows it.** Every arena's D lies above the training floor, and every arena sits below every training map in zero-shot skill for all three backbones (U-Net 1.14 to 1.96 against 2.55 to 3.56). The figure that gives Rohan his sentence is D on x and the three backbones' zero-shot skill on y, training maps included: a step, not a slope. Astra adds the home-to-fresh scene-PSNR drops beside it (2.37 / 2.20 / 2.69 dB for U-Net / PixArt / SD 3.5).
4. **A model-side "distance" that orders the arenas exists**, and both name the same construction from different directions: the model's zero-shot latent error after calibrating away what motion and control explain on the source. Opus computed it as Δ, the motion-matched skill deficit (per window, home skill in the same copy-last-error decile minus the arena's skill; floor passes for all three backbones; ρ −0.90 with A4000, +0.65 with the budget; cross-backbone 0.92 and 0.88). Astra's candidate 2, the calibrated residual excess (ridge on log-motion and control indicators fitted on source folds), is the same quantity with a fitted rather than binned calibration. Both say plainly: it needs the model, it re-scales S0 (ρ −0.89 with S0), and on these arenas it is exploratory.
5. **Method.** Report A at the budget and the area under the curve as the primary outcomes and keep the half-gap cost as secondary with its censoring stated (Harrell's C in the appendix: S0 0.73, A0 0.56, D 0.36). Put arena-bootstrap intervals on every ρ and a power sentence (0.37 at a true ρ of 0.5, n = 13). Promote the 8k set to the headline once it is rescored through the same decoder with its own guards, without splicing the 4k trajectories. Do not spend on new encoders, FVD/DINO/JEPA distances, rank sweeps or more episodes.
6. **October.** Constructed shifts that hold difficulty fixed: retextured training maps (geometry, bots and spawns fixed; XEWorld's control), layout-varied generated arenas with training textures stratified by D before recording, and other-WAD maps for range; sealed test maps, two seeds, frozen predictors. 80 to 110 A4000-hours.

## Where they differ

- **Seeds.** Opus: more seeds are not worth it. Astra: rerun seed 1 on the four pilot arenas on the new grid (the old seed-1 runs certify the 4k grid, not the 8k one). Resolution: Astra's, because it is cheap (4 runs, about 2.3 h on four cards after the chain) and the 8k set otherwise carries a seed claim it did not measure.
- **Full fine-tuning.** Astra: arenas 12 and 7 only (8 to 12 A6000-hours), fit-check first. Rohan's standing direction is all 13 on Monday; keep it, since the comparator column is per arena.
- **Wording.** Astra wants the S0 result called an exploratory diagnostic; Opus reads it as the finding of the study ("difficulty, not shift, orders the arenas"). Resolution: the paper states it as a finding with n = 13, the cross-backbone agreement and the development-data caveat in the same sentence.
