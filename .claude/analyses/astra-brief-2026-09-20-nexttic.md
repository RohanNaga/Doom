## The problem
We train action-conditioned latent diffusion world models of Doom (SD 1.4 U-Net, DiT-XL/2, PixArt-alpha, UniDiffuser, SD 3.5 Medium) under one recipe and they all land about 2 dB above the copy-last-frame floor (21.1 to 21.4 dB PSNR teacher-forced on training maps, floor 19.4; GameNGen reports 29.43). Rohan's goal for the next round: a model that is stronger than GameNGen, shown honestly. He has decided the next runs predict the NEXT TIC (35 Hz), not the next agent decision (every 4th tic) as all our rows do today, on the new dense corpus, for the SD 1.4 U-Net and SD 3.5 first and possibly a third backbone.

## Facts we have established (each could be wrong; check what you doubt)
- GameNGen's open reproduction trains on consecutive tics with the agent action repeated for 4 tics (code-verified); GameNGen most likely does too ("35FPS data", App. A.6) though the paper never states its stride.
- Persistence floors, same instrument: reproduction footage 23.8 dB at 1 tic, 21.2 at 4; our Arnold deathmatch footage 21.6 at 1 tic, 18.9 at 4. Stock SD 1.x autoencoder ceiling 24.4 (theirs) vs 23.6 (ours); our LPIPS-tuned 4-channel decoder reaches 28.6; our tuned SD 3.5 16-channel decoder 31.7.
- GameNGen's frozen-decoder ablation scores 22.4 dB; with its decoder fine-tune (MSE only, batch 2,048, as many steps as the denoiser) 29.4 headline and 32.6 in its sampling-steps table (DDIM 4 steps, CFG 1.5 on the context, context dropout 0.1).
- GameNGen Figure 13: 1M examples peaks at 25.9 dB at 20k steps x batch 128 then overfits; 10M reaches 28.6 by 400k steps; 70M reaches 29.4 at 700k; all curves coincide below about 100k steps. Our rows: about 1.05M examples, 90k steps x batch 32.
- Our recipe: v-prediction, linear betas, lr 5e-5 AdamW with 2k warmup, batch 32, 32 context latents channel-stacked, single most recent action as the condition, GameNGen context-noise augmentation (max 0.7, 10 buckets, level given to the model), EMA 0.9999, DDIM 50 at evaluation, no CFG. Grid cell `noaug` (PixArt 30k, augmentation off) beat the base cell teacher-forced by 0.3 dB and 0.02 LPIPS with equal rollout PSNR, better action fidelity, worse FVD (219/469 vs 186/379). Adding noise at inference never helps. SD 3.5 needed more than 50k updates to pass the 4-channel rows (40k: behind; 70k: ahead on unseen maps and LPIPS).
- Data now on disk, every tic, lossless: arenas 2 to 5, 8,000 episodes, 40.3M tics; arenas 6 to 8, 3,000 episodes, 14.8M tics (the Arnold paper's own train/test maps); original 17-map corpus 850 episodes, 4.2M tics; seeded evaluation corpora (seen 60 episodes, unseen 20, unseen-13-maps 130).
