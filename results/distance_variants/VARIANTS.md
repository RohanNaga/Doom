## Definitions and configuration

- **D (motion-weighted SW2)**: sliced W2, motion-weighted, nearest training map; the frozen D.
- **SW2 (unweighted)**: sliced W2, every frame weighted equally, nearest training map.
- **Frechet (FID formula)**: ||m1 - m2||^2 + tr(S1 + S2 - 2 (S1 S2)^1/2) of Gaussian fits, nearest map.
- **Gaussian KL(target||source)**: KL between Gaussian fits, target to source, nearest map.
- **Gaussian KL(source||target)**: KL between Gaussian fits, source to target, nearest map.
- **MMD^2 (Gaussian kernel)**: unbiased MMD^2, bandwidth the median pairwise distance of the reference.
- **kNN KL(target||source)**: Wang-Kulkarni-Verdu kNN KL estimator, k = 5, target to source.
- **kNN KL(source||target)**: Wang-Kulkarni-Verdu kNN KL estimator, k = 5, source to target.
- **SW2 to the pooled corpus**: motion-weighted sliced W2 to all four maps pooled, equal shares.
- **Frechet to the pooled corpus**: Frechet distance to the Gaussian fit of all four maps.

- Clouds: 10 clouds of 4 episodes per target map (250 frames per episode), seeded as `distance_study.py distances` seeds them; references are the four training maps' full draw-1 clouds; the floor is 10 draw-2 clouds per training map against the same references, nearest map.
- Sliced variants: 1000 directions, seed 0, `distance_study.distance_table`.
- MMD bandwidth: 5.5445, the median pairwise distance of 5000 seeded frames of the pooled draw-1 reference, one bandwidth for every comparison.
- Covariances (192-d): condition numbers up to 2.26e+04 for the 1,000-frame clouds (median 7.39e+03) and 4.71e+03 to 1.02e+04 for the references; no ridge was added.
- Reproduction: D, the unweighted SW2 and the pooled SW2 match the frozen tables to 2.4e-16 over 17 maps and the 40 floor clouds, with identical episode subsets (pass).
- Nearest-map agreement with D (share of target clouds whose nearest training map is D's): SW2 (unweighted) 0.95, Frechet (FID formula) 0.85, Gaussian KL(target||source) 0.42, Gaussian KL(source||target) 0.37, MMD^2 (Gaussian kernel) 0.94, kNN KL(target||source) 0.84, kNN KL(source||target) 0.47.
