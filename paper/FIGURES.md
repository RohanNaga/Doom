## Draft captions (figure lead, 2026-09-27; for the paper owner to paste and trim)

Each caption states the finding first, then how to read the marks, then provenance. Terms follow item 13 of `paper/diffs/2026-09-27-sunday-adaptation.md`: persistence (the copy-last baseline, the zero line), in-distribution, non-EMA, budget, reconstruction upper bound, $d$ for the frame distance, fine-tuned decoder, control checks. Unless a caption says otherwise, $A$ is the one-tic scene-crop (rows 0 to 207) PSNR of the decoded prediction minus that of decoded persistence against the decoded truth (dB, "ΔPSNR vs persistence" on the axes). $M$ is LPIPS(prediction, raw frame) minus LPIPS(persistence, raw frame), where lower is better (the sign switch `figstyle.M_POSITIVE_IS_BETTER` is off). $A_\text{train}$ is $A$ on the training maps' 512 validation windows (99 episodes). Numbers are from `paper/tables/tuned/adapt_summary.json` and the sidecars named below; they must be re-read after the 8k rerun.

**Figure 1a, method** (`fig1_method.tex`, owned by opus-method-fixes). We build a 17-arena Doom benchmark: 4 training maps with 500 episodes each and 13 unseen arenas with 24 episodes each. We train three pretrained image backbones under one next-tic recipe, fine-tune the decoder on the training maps, and adapt the U-Net to a new arena with a rank-16 adapter. The context and the noisy next latent are stacked on channels, and the model predicts the next tic from 32 true tics and the 19 executed buttons. A 10-step DDIM sampler turns the predicted $v$ into the next latent. The decoder is frozen at evaluation and is not part of the dynamics. We score $A$, $M$ and the directional score; $G$ (gap to the reconstruction upper bound) and the zero-shot latent skill $S_0$ are defined in Section 3. The Arnold agent plays against 8 bots in 150 s episodes; parameter counts are in Table 1.

**Figure 1b, training** (`fig1_curves.pdf`, 1.5 in, EMA only; the full-width `figA_training_curves.pdf` adds the non-EMA weights dotted). Every backbone ends above persistence on the training maps: the U-Net by +0.92 dB and PixArt-α by +0.91 dB at 200k updates, and SD 3.5 by +1.75 dB at 155k. Lines are EMA weights; open markers every 50k mark reads through the stock decoders; the unjoined SD 3.5 marker is a provisional 170k read from another scorer. Each point is the mean one-tic full-frame PSNR minus raw persistence (21.57 dB) on the 512 training-map validation windows (10-step DDIM, seed 0, raw frames). The reads are means without intervals. EMA reads below −1 dB (5k to 20k updates, 6.7 to 20.4 dB PSNR, while the 0.9999 EMA still averages the pretrained start) are clipped at the axis floor.

**Figure 2, rollouts** (`fig2_rollouts.pdf`, `fig2_rollouts.json`). On arena 7 the camera turns the right way while the walls and floor drift toward the training maps' grey stone; after eight adaptation episodes and 4k updates the rollout keeps the arena's red floor and white walls. Under a held left turn, the zero-shot U-Net's scene PSNR falls from 21.4 dB at tic 1 to 14.6 dB by tic 8, and the adapted model's holds 23.3 dB through tic 4. Both still fall by tic 32 (14.5 and 13.0 dB), where persistence scores 15.1 dB. On map 2 (training) the U-Net stays above persistence at every tic shown. Column 0 is the last true context frame, which is also persistence's prediction at every tic; columns are tics +1 to +32 of a 32-tic closed-loop rollout from 32 true tics under the recorded (held) control. Model rows use the fine-tuned decoder, true rows the raw frame, all cropped to the scene rows 0 to 207. Grey numbers are each model frame's scene PSNR against the raw frame. U-Net 200k EMA, rank-16 adapter after 4k updates (non-EMA), 10-step sampler, seed 0. Windows: map 2 episode 6000 from tic 837 (turn left) and 225 (forward); arena 7 episode 41 from tic 269 and 209.

**Figure 3a, the family step** (`fig2d_family_step.pdf`). The shift is a step, not a slope: for all three backbones every unseen arena has lower zero-shot latent skill than every training map (margins 0.59, 0.24 and 0.63 dB), and among the arenas skill does not follow the frame distance $d$ (Spearman −0.05, −0.15, −0.17). Each marker is one map (4 training, 13 unseen) and one backbone. The grey band is the train-versus-train $d$ floor [0.022, 0.093], inside which the training maps lie. Horizontal segments are each backbone's median over the training maps and over the arenas, drawn across each family's $d$ range. No line is fitted. $S_0$ is the mean over windows of −10 log10 of the model's latent MSE over persistence's; each arena has 256 windows (8 held-out episodes) and the training maps have their validation windows. U-Net and PixArt-α are at 200k EMA; SD 3.5 is a provisional 170k EMA read.

**Figure 3b, zero-shot per arena with both decoders** (`fig3b_zero_shot_paired.pdf`). Off the training maps every backbone keeps a positive $A$ but loses the perceptual edge it has at home: $M$ is above zero on 13 of 13 arenas for all three backbones, while it is below zero on the training maps. The fine-tuned decoder raises $A$ (U-Net median +0.22 dB, on 12 of 13 arenas; PixArt-α +0.25 dB, on 13 of 13) and lowers $M$ on 13 of 13, but it does not close the gap to the training maps. Arenas are ordered by the U-Net's zero-shot latent skill $S_0$; the first column holds each backbone's read on the training maps (in-distribution). Colour and marker identify the backbone. Open markers use the stock decoder and filled markers the fine-tuned decoder, on the same windows, joined by a grey segment; within an arena the backbones are offset on purpose. SD 3.5 has no fine-tuned decoder yet (open only). The dark diamonds are the U-Net after 4k adapter updates through the fine-tuned decoder. Vertical lines are 95% episode-bootstrap intervals. Each arena has 256 windows; one tic; scene rows 0 to 207.

**Figure 3c, one latent through both decoders** (drawn from `results/decoder_pair/`; not composed yet). The fine-tuned decoder repairs the HUD and sharpens the scene of the same predicted latent. On map 2 (forward, tic +1) the scene PSNR rises from 26.7 to 27.9 dB and the HUD from 17.9 to 30.4 dB; on arena 7 (forward) the scene rises from 20.4 to 20.6 dB and the HUD from 18.1 to 29.6 dB. The columns are the stock decoder, the fine-tuned decoder and the raw frame, with the HUD kept.

**Figure 4, adaptation** (`fig4_adaptation.pdf`; round 2 chooses between 4a and `fig4a_gapshare.pdf`). Eight episodes and 4k adapter updates lift the interquartile mean (IQM, the mean of the middle half of the arenas) of $A$ from 2.03 dB [1.69, 2.38] to 3.79 dB [3.52, 4.27], most of it by 250 updates (a). By 4k, 9 of 13 arenas (0.69 [0.46, 0.92]) have closed half of their gap to the in-distribution reference; arenas 1, 8, 12 and 16 are censored at 4k, 0.08 to 0.15 dB short (b). (a) Heavy line and band: the IQM across the 13 arenas and its pointwise 95% nested bootstrap band (10,000 draws, arenas then each arena's held-out episodes, paired across budgets). Faint lines: the individual arenas, with 7, 9 and 12 labelled. The grey band and dashed line are the training maps (in-distribution), $A_\text{train}$ = 5.06 dB [4.75, 5.38] (episode bootstrap). This is a reference, not the arena's own ceiling. (b) Solid step curve: the fraction of arenas whose $A$ has reached its half-gap line $(A_0 + A_\text{train})/2$, one minus Kaplan-Meier with every censoring at the last read, and its band. The dashed curve is the fixed threshold $A \geq 3.5$ dB with its bounds dotted; it coincides with the half-gap curve except at 1k (6 against 7 arenas). The filled band belongs to the half-gap curve. Ticks above the curves mark each arena's first crossing (open: censored at 4k). The row under the axis counts the arenas at risk just before each read. U-Net 200k EMA source, rank-16 adapter, 8 adaptation episodes per arena, non-EMA adapter weights, seed 0, fine-tuned decoder, 256 held-out windows per arena, one tic, 10-step DDIM, scene rows 0 to 207. All claims are about these 13 arenas of one WAD.

**Figure 4a, gap-share variant** (`fig4a_gapshare.pdf`). The adapters close about half of each arena's gap to the in-distribution reference: the IQM share rises to 0.41 by 250 updates and 0.61 [0.51, 0.73] by 4k. The y axis is $(A - A_0)/(A_\text{train} - A_0)$ per arena; the dotted line at 0.5 is the half-gap criterion and the dashed line at 1 is the training maps. Band, faint lines and provenance are as in Figure 4a.

**Appendix: per-arena adaptation** (`figA_adapt_arenas.pdf`). Gains differ between arenas, and the four arenas that miss their half-gap line by 4k do so by at most 0.15 dB. Each panel shows one arena's $A$ against adapter updates, with diamonds at the measured budgets and the band giving the 95% episode-bootstrap interval (8 held-out episodes). The dotted line is the arena's half-gap line and the tick its first crossing; the key in the empty slot names the reference marks. Arenas 1, 8, 12 and 16 are censored at 4k: the missing tick means no crossing within the grid, not missing data. Provenance as in Figure 4.

**Appendix: performance profiles** (`figA_adapt_profiles.pdf`). Adaptation shifts the whole arena distribution upward, but only one arena exceeds the training maps at 4k. At 4k all 13 arenas are above 3.0 dB, 9 are above 3.5 and 4 are above 4.0. Each curve is the fraction of the 13 arenas with $A \geq \tau$ at 0 (zero-shot), 500 and 4k updates, shaded light to dark by budget, with pointwise nested 95% bands. The black line at 0 is persistence, and the grey band is the training maps (in-distribution). Provenance as in Figure 4.

**Appendix: A at 4k against zero-shot skill** (`fig2c_outcomes_by_skill.pdf`). Where an arena ends is ordered by its zero-shot latent skill (Spearman 0.90, 95% interval [0.53, 0.94] with $A$ resampled by arena and episode); how much it gains is not (0.18). One diamond per arena carries its 95% episode interval, and markers drawn on top of each other share one label. The training maps are the grey band. Provenance as in Figure 4.

**Appendix: A at 4k against the skill deficit** (`fig2e_deficit.pdf`, from `tools/family_step.py`). The motion-matched skill deficit $\Delta$ orders the arenas' endpoints (Spearman −0.90) and their budgets (+0.62); it passes the training maps' floor, and $d$ does not. $\Delta$ is the U-Net's mean skill shortfall against the training maps' windows of matched persistence error (10 quantile bins). Otherwise as in the $S_0$ panel.

**Appendix: seeds** (`fig3_adaptation_seeds.pdf`, stock decoder until the seed-1 fine-tuned rows land). A second training seed moves $A$ at 4k by 0.02 to 0.06 dB on arenas 6, 7, 8 and 16, about a tenth of the spread between arenas. (a) Each arena's curve for seed 0 (solid) and seed 1 (dashed). (b) Seed 1 minus seed 0 per budget, with dotted lines at the largest spread at 4k. Same adaptation episodes; stock decoder; otherwise as in Figure 4.

**Appendix: data ladder** (`fig_adapt_ladder.pdf`, stock decoder). One adaptation episode already gives most of the sixteen-episode endpoint: $A$ at 4k with 1 against 16 episodes is 3.20 against 3.43 dB (arena 7), 2.87 against 2.86 (8), 2.31 against 2.56 (12) and 2.95 against 3.00 (16). Dotted lines mark each arena's step-0 $A$. The four arenas were chosen before the runs, so there is no population interval.

**Appendix: recipe tests** (`fig_adapt_recipe.pdf`, stock decoder). Nothing in the recipe moves the endpoint. Learning rates of 3e-4 and 5e-4 and the 8k grid change $A$ at 4k by at most +0.06 dB on arenas 12 and 16, within the seed spread. Black is the base recipe (lr 1e-4) and light grey its seed-1 run.
