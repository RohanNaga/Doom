## Draft captions (figure lead, round 2, 2026-09-27; for the paper owner to paste and trim)

Each caption states the finding first, then how to read the marks, then provenance. The terms follow item 13 of `paper/diffs/2026-09-27-sunday-adaptation.md`: persistence (the copy-last baseline), in-distribution, non-EMA, budget, reconstruction upper bound, $d$ for the frame distance, fine-tuned decoder, control checks. Unless a caption says otherwise, $A$ ("ΔPSNR vs persistence" on the axes) is the one-tic, scene-crop (rows 0 to 207) PSNR of the decoded prediction minus that of decoded persistence, both against the decoded truth, in dB. $M$ is LPIPS(prediction, raw frame) minus LPIPS(persistence, raw frame); lower is better while `figstyle.M_POSITIVE_IS_BETTER` is off. $A_\text{train}$ is $A$ on the training maps' 512 validation windows (99 episodes): 5.06 dB [4.75, 5.38], an episode-bootstrap interval. It is an in-distribution reference, not an arena's own ceiling. Numbers come from `paper/tables/tuned/adapt_summary.json`, `results/family_step/family_step.json` and the figures' JSON sidecars, and must be re-read after the 8k rerun.

**Figure 1a, method** (`fig1_method.tex`, owned by opus-method-fixes). We build a 17-arena Doom benchmark: 4 training maps with 500 episodes each and 13 unseen arenas with 24 episodes each. We train three pretrained image backbones under one next-tic recipe, fine-tune the decoder on the training maps, and adapt the U-Net to a new arena with a rank-16 adapter. The context and the noisy next latent are stacked on channels, and a 10-step DDIM sampler turns the predicted $v$ into the next latent. The decoder is frozen at evaluation and is not part of the dynamics. We score $A$, $M$ and the directional check; $G$ (gap to the reconstruction upper bound) and the zero-shot latent skill $S_0$ are defined in Section 3. The Arnold agent plays against 8 bots in 150 s episodes; parameter counts are in Table 1.

Each point is the mean one-tic full-frame PSNR minus raw persistence (21.57 dB) on the 512 training-map validation windows, with EMA weights, stock decoders, raw frames, a 10-step DDIM sampler and seed 0. ## Figure 1 (method)

File: `figures/fig1_method.pdf`, 5.5 x 1.8 in, included at scale 1 (`\includegraphics{figures/fig1_method.pdf}`); source `figures/fig1_method.tex`, images `figures/assets/fig1_*.png`. Terms follow the Sunday diff's terminology table (section 13 of `paper/diffs/2026-09-27-sunday-adaptation.md`).

Caption (LaTeX-ready):

> **Method.** We build a next-tic benchmark on the 17 arenas of one Doom deathmatch WAD, train three pretrained image-diffusion backbones as world models under one training configuration, and post-train twice: the world model adapts to an unseen arena from 8 episodes, and the decoder that renders every prediction is fine-tuned. (a) The Arnold agent plays against 8 bots in 150\,s episodes; every tic (one game step at 35\,Hz) records a $320\times240$ frame $x_t$ and the 19 buttons $u_t$ the game executed. The 4 training maps have 500 episodes each; the 13 unseen arenas have 24 each (16 for adaptation, 8 held out). The frozen encoder $E$ of the backbone's autoencoder maps each frame to a latent $z_t$ ($4\times32\times40$ for SD\,1.x latents, $16\times32\times40$ for SD\,3.5). (b) The 32 context latents, noised to a level $q<0.7$ whose bucket $b$ (one of 10) is an input, and the noisy next latent are stacked on channels; the 32 executed controls enter through an MLP with learned positions. The denoiser is one of three pretrained backbones; its $v$-prediction $\hat v$ goes through a 10-step DDIM sampler to the predicted next latent $\hat z_{t+1}$. Each backbone trains for 200k updates of batch 32 (AdamW, learning rate $5\times10^{-5}$, fp32 EMA 0.9999). (c) The adapter is a rank-16 LoRA on the U-Net's 200k EMA weights, with the control path trained in full (4.2M trained parameters, 0.49\%), fit for 4k updates to 8 episodes of one arena; the decoder fine-tune starts from the stock decoder $D$ and fits it to training-map frames from their ground-truth latents with MSE + 0.1 LPIPS. (d) One tic ahead from 32 ground-truth context tics with a clean context (bucket 0): for each backbone, the prediction $\hat z_{t+1}$, the ground-truth next latent $z_{t+1}$ and the persistence baseline $z_t$ (the last context frame copied forward) are all rendered by the one fine-tuned decoder $D$, and scored on scene rows 0 to 207 (the HUD is cropped). $A$ is the PSNR of the rendered prediction minus that of rendered persistence, both against the rendered ground-truth frame (ΔPSNR vs persistence, dB); $M$ is the LPIPS of the rendered prediction against the raw ground-truth frame minus that of raw persistence (lower is better); the directional check swaps the newest turn control (left for right) and asks whether the predicted view turns the other way. The gap $G$ to the reconstruction upper bound and the zero-shot latent skill score $S_0$ are defined in the text. Unseen arenas are scored on 256 held-out windows each, the training maps on 512 validation windows. The three outlined boxes give each backbone the colour and name it keeps in every later figure (U-Net blue, PixArt-$\alpha$ vermillion, SD\,3.5 green). Images are ground-truth frames with the HUD cropped: (a) map 2, validation episode 6024; the stacks, map 2 (validation episode 6000) and arena 7 (episodes 41 and 314). The pressed cells of the button bar (forward, attack) are illustrative, not that frame's recorded control. U-Net and PixArt-$\alpha$ share the SD\,1.x latents and its fine-tuned decoder; SD\,3.5 has its own, whose fine-tune is \prov{pending (its reads so far use the stock decoder)}.
