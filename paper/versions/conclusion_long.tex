\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:limits}

The claims cover 13 unseen maps of one game, one agent, one-tic scoring and mostly one seed.
One-tic quality does not guarantee stable rollouts (\appref{app:closedloop}).
Next are other Doom maps and other games, to see whether the same procedure transfers; detecting a shift from the model's own zero-shot scores so that it repairs itself; closed-loop evaluation of the adapted models; and agents trained inside the adapted model.
The pattern we measure, control kept, appearance lost, most of it relearned from a few episodes, is what a deployed world model would need to detect and repair on its own, and DoomShift is a testbed for that loop.
